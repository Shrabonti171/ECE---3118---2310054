## 1. _Purpose of the System_

<p>
The main purpose of this system is to make departmental management easier, more organized and efficient.

The system aims to:

1 . Maintain student admission and personal information. <br>
2 . Manage student enrollment in different courses.<br>
3 . Organize course and credit information.<br>
4 . Maintain instructor records.<br>
5 . Manage academic results.<br>
6 . Organize class and examination schedules.<br>
7 . Maintain semester-related information.<br>
8 . Reduce manual paperwork and improve data accuracy.<br>
</p>

## 2. _Elements of the System_
<p>
The system consists of the following main elements (modules):

### 01. Student Enrollment
Stores student admission information, including admission ID, admission date, academic session and enrollment status.

### 02. Student Information
Maintains students' personal information, such as student ID, name, contact details, email, address and academic session.

### 03. Course Enrollment
Manages students' registration and enrollment in different courses for each semester.

### 04. Course Credits
Stores course information, including course code, course title, credit hours, course type and semester.

### 05. Instructor Information
Maintains instructor information, including instructor ID, name, designation, email and contact details.

### 06. Result
Stores and manages students' academic results, including course grades, GPA and semester-wise performance.

### 07. Class Schedule Information
Maintains class routine details, including course name, instructor, classroom, class day and class time.

### 08. Exam Schedule
Stores examination information, including exam date, time, course code, examination room and exam type.

### 09. Semester Information
Maintains semester details, including semester name, academic year, starting date, ending date and semester status.

</p>
